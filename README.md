## About Us

Vector Stock Exchange is a cutting-edge stock exchange platform that empowers individuals and institutions to trade with confidence. We provide a comprehensive marketplace where companies can list their stocks and brokerages can seamlessly connect to execute trades on behalf of their clients.

Our platform supports all types of securities, offering unparalleled flexibility and choice for traders and investors. With round-the-clock trading capabilities (24/7, excluding market halts), Vector Stock Exchange ensures you never miss an opportunity.
